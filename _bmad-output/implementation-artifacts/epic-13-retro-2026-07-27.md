## Gaps, Inconsistencies & Risks Audit

*(requested focus: incongruencies, inconsistencies, gaps, technical debt, and risks)*

### 1. [Critical] `[REPEAT 4x]` Epic sequencing violated again: Epic 14 retro'd while Epic 13 was still in-flight — the exact pattern A12-1 was supposed to close

`sprint-status.yaml` shows `epic-13-retrospective: optional` (never run) while `epic-14-retrospective:
done`, dated 2026-07-26. Git log proves the interleaving directly: Epic 14's retro commits
(`d158087`/`bc9f286`, 2026-07-26 20:10:00) landed **2 minutes after** Epic 13's final story (`13-4`)
was even created (`3846e9b`, 20:08:38), and roughly 30 hours **before** `13-4` merged (`15a599a`,
2026-07-27 02:05:52). `epic-14-retro-2026-07-26.md` Step 7 self-acknowledges the drift: *"Epic 13 is
next per the recommended build order (14 → 13 → 17 → 15 → 16) and is already in-progress in
parallel."*

This recurs from **P6-1/P7-1/P8-1's sibling pattern** P6-2/P7-2/P8-3 (epics 6, 7, 8) and was
"fixed" as **A12-1** in Epic 12's retro just three days before this one (2026-07-24): the `pick-story`
A0 guard was rewritten to scan all prior epics for the highest-numbered one with completed-but-unretro'd
work, replacing a fragile N-1-only check. That fix works for its stated case — but it only guards
against a **new epic starting** while an earlier one sits done-but-unretro'd. It has no logic at all
for the mirror case: an **already-started, lower-numbered epic remaining open** while a **later
epic finishes and retros**. That's precisely what happened here. This is the **fourth** occurrence of
this general failure shape, and the **second one this fix specifically failed to prevent** despite
being written to prevent it.

**Resolution:** Routed to Step 4 option 5 (tooling/skill guard) — **edited this session**, since a
skill-file edit is cheap and immediate and prose describing this exact needed edit had already gone
nowhere twice before. Added a new **Step 1.4 "Check for Lower-Numbered Epics Still In-Progress"**
guard to `~/.claude/skills/my-epic-retro/SKILL.md`: before a retro runs for epic N, it now scans for
any epic M<N with at least one non-`backlog`/non-`done` story (started but not finished) and
surfaces a warning before proceeding, rather than silently letting the retro run. This is the mirror
check to `pick-story`'s A0 — A0 covers *starting* a new epic while an earlier one sits
done-but-unretro'd; this new step covers *retro'ing* an epic while an earlier one is still
in-progress. Between the two, both directions of the P6-2/P7-2/P8-3 shape are now guarded.

### 2. [High] `[REPEAT — new instance of A12-3's underlying gap]` Phantom forward reference: "Story 13.5" cited twice as the owner of a known limitation, but no such story exists anywhere

Story `13-2`'s sprint-status entry and Dev Notes explicitly defer "a rotation same-value check
limitation" to "Story 13.5's territory." `epics.md` defines only Stories 13.1–13.4 (`grep -n "Story
13\."` confirms); `sprint-status.yaml` has no `13-5` key. Unlike the properly-scheduled `14-5` pattern
from Epic 14's own retro (a real backlog entry closing a real gap), "Story 13.5" is pure narrative
debt — it survived the `bmad-dev-story` P4 checklist item (added in Epic 12's retro specifically to
catch untracked deferrals) because the checklist's phrasing looks for "known limitation" language, not
forward references to a story number that was never created.

**Resolution:** Routed to Step 4 as a follow-up story option below (option 1/2). The P4 checklist gap
itself is a smaller, non-blocking documentation fix worth a note in Action Items, not a full story.

### 3. [High] Two explicit 13-4 scope exclusions have no tracked follow-up anywhere

Story `13-4` documents two deliberate judgment calls in its Dev Notes: dependency-creation
`field_key` UI/API is "explicitly out of scope for this story," and "a single `targetFields` request
applies the same `newValue` to every selected field... documented here since the story text doesn't
explicitly rule on the multi-field-different-values case." Neither has a corresponding row in
`sprint-status.yaml` or a stub in `epics.md` (confirmed via grep for `field_key` and
"newValue"/"per-field value map" across both files). Both are functionally safe today (conservative
defaults), but both are real product gaps sitting only in prose.

**Resolution:** Routed to Step 4 below — can be folded into the same follow-up story as Finding 2, or
split, per user preference once available.

### 4. [Medium] `13-1`'s missing `ON DELETE` clause on `credentials.current_version_id` — accepted trade-off with no revisit trigger

Migration `0049_credentials_current_version_id_backfill.sql` adds
`current_version_id uuid REFERENCES credential_versions(id)` with no `ON DELETE` behavior
(`packages/db/src/schema/credentials.ts` confirms no `.onDelete(...)`). `13-1`'s own code review left
this "unfixed as out-of-scope... inert today since nothing hard-deletes credentials/versions yet" —
correct today, but nothing (no `deferred-work.md` row, no explicit trigger condition) will catch this
before a future hard-delete feature silently leaves a dangling/blocking FK behind.

**Resolution:** Tracked as technical debt (Step 4 option 3) with a stated trigger: revisit when any
hard-delete path for credentials/credential_versions is implemented.

### 5. [Low] Migration `0055` breaks the inline-documentation convention `0049` set earlier in the same epic

rules, scale notes). `0055_rotation_target_fields_and_dependency_field_key.sql` is two bare `ALTER
TABLE` statements with zero comments, even though the design rationale is extensively documented
elsewhere (`architecture.md:333`, the `13-4` story file). Not a functional issue — a
documentation-locality inconsistency within Epic 13's own migrations.

**Resolution:** Accepted as-is. Low severity, not worth a tracked item; noted here so a future reader
of the migrations folder isn't confused by the inconsistency.
