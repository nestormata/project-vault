### Challenges and growth areas

- The first final CI attempt failed on stale Epic 18 metadata even though its child stories and
  retrospective were already done. This was a tracking hygiene failure, not an application defect;
  the rollup was corrected and the second full run passed.
- Browser setup required explicit environment discipline. J7 used a DB fixture because the real
  project-create path is rate-limited; J10 required the real Settings → Language path because an API
  locale update alone does not establish the SSR cookie. These are now documented in the story
  evidence rather than hidden as unexplained retries.
- The guidance checker is intentionally source-convention-based and narrow. It currently scans
  native controls and documented structural exclusions; future custom control patterns must either
  be covered by the checker or have an explicit reviewed exclusion.

### 2. [Low] Review-deferral guard depends on the documented review-bullet shape

The new guard is deterministic and green, but its contract is intentionally narrow:
`scripts/check-story-review-deferrals.ts:7-8` says it scans unchecked `[Review]` bullets with explicit
deferral language, and the matcher is defined at `scripts/check-story-review-deferrals.ts:27`.
If future review records move deferrals into a different prose shape, the guard could miss them
without a corresponding test-fixture update. The current Epic 19 stories have no unchecked review
deferral, and `deferred-work.md` tracks the four Epic 19 follow-through items.
**Resolution:** Accepted as contained tooling debt. Treat any change to review-record format as a
maintenance trigger: update the checker and its fixtures in the same change. No follow-up story is
needed and no override is recorded.

### 3. [Informational] Cross-file, product-surface, and invariant checks are clean
